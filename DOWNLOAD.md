# The Hollow Crown — Android alpha 0.2.0

[Download the native Android APK](https://github.com/Jhon-from-the-501st/Game-rts-rpg/raw/refs/heads/android-downloads/the-hollow-crown-android.apk)

Open the link in Chrome on your Android phone. On the GitHub file page, **Download raw file** also downloads the APK. Supports Android 7+, ARM64 and x86_64, in landscape.

Install over alpha 0.1.0 to preserve your campaign. Both versions use the same app ID and development signing certificate. Do not uninstall first. Existing campaigns gain roaming parties automatically.

## New in 0.2.0

- 28 hostile bands with patrol, hunt, pursuit, search, caravan interception, settlement raiding, retreat, and recovery objectives.
- 27 persistent traders that compare markets, buy and sell cargo, follow roads, evade danger, and reform after losses. Aggregate production/consumption and regional market matching continue independently.
- Tap caravans to trade or escort them for 45 crowns and 2 renown per safe arrival. Nearby escorts strengthen their protection. A trader starts near the new company.
- Bounded map panning, anchored pinch zoom, full-world overview, camera-aware minimap, smooth fog, new pixel party sprites, dusk lighting, and settlement lanterns.
- Title/continue/new-campaign menus, tactical pause menu, mobile touch targets, background pause, and 30/45/60 FPS choices.
- GPU-compressed terrain, cached road graph, staggered world brains, and less frequent fog/combat target updates.

101 native gameplay checks and full UI smoke checks pass, including multi-day trade/raid simulation and old-save migration. Development-signed playable alpha; physical phone frame rate, battery and thermal behavior still need device testing. Full empire diplomacy and terrain-aware pathfinding are future work.
